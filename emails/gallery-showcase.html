Range and Restore is based at 130 Junction Road, Archway, two minutes on foot from Archway tube. The clinic is set up for focused, assessment-led care, with a dedicated treatment room and the new Recovery Suite on site.

The Recovery Suite adds infrared sauna and Normatec compression therapy to the existing sports massage and soft tissue services. Sessions can be booked on their own or combined into a full recovery appointment. Every treatment, whatever the length, starts with a proper assessment.
Common questions

What treatment length should I book for my first session?

For a first visit I recommend a 60 or 90 minute session. That gives us time to assess your posture, talk through your needs, and do proper hands-on treatment.

What should I wear?

Loose, comfortable clothing. Shorts for lower body work, a vest or sports bra for upper body. You are professionally draped at all times and only the area being treated is exposed.

Do I need to be an athlete?

Not at all. Sports massage helps anyone with muscular discomfort, desk-related stiffness, poor posture or stress, not just runners and athletes.

Common questions

Do I need a GP referral for the scan?

No. Reliable Scan takes self-referrals, so there is no GP letter or insurance paperwork needed. If you have previous scans or medical letters, bring them along.

Can the scan and massage happen on the same day?

Where availability allows, yes. Both run at 130 Junction Road, scan first, then treatment. If not, the scan is booked first so the report is ready before your massage.

Do I have to arrange the ultrasound myself?

No. Once your booking comes through, a member of the team contacts you to help arrange the scan with Reliable Scan. You are also welcome to contact them directly.

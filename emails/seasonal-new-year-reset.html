Most people only book a massage when something hurts. That is understandable, but it means they spend a good portion of the year managing discomfort rather than preventing it. A regular session, even once every four to six weeks, makes a measurable difference to how your body feels day to day.

January is a good time to reset that habit, particularly if the Christmas period left you stiffer, less active, or carrying more tension than usual.
Blue Monday · 19 January 2026

The third Monday in January

Whether or not you put stock in the calendar, the third week of January is genuinely one of the harder weeks of the year. Short days, grey skies, and the weight of returning to routine all take a physical toll, not just a psychological one.

Tension in the neck, shoulders, and lower back tends to build quietly during periods of stress or reduced movement. A session around this time is a straightforward way to address what has accumulated and start February feeling considerably better than January deserves.

Why a spring session makes sense
Pre-event preparation

Spring is when most running and cycling events land. A session before your target event helps identify and address what is tight or restricted before it becomes a problem on the day.
Winter stiffness

Months of heavier clothing, colder weather, and reduced movement builds up. A proper deep-tissue session can clear that stiffness and restore range in a single appointment.
Recovery Suite reset

The infrared sauna is particularly good for general fatigue and joint stiffness. A 45-minute session, solo or combined with a massage, is a strong way to start the spring.

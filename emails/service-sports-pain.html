Common questions

Is sports massage supposed to hurt?

There can be some discomfort when working through tight tissue, but it should always be manageable. I check in throughout. Any soreness usually settles within 24 to 48 hours.

What is the difference between sports and deep-tissue massage?

Sports massage is geared to performance, recovery and injury prevention. Deep-tissue work targets chronic tightness in deeper muscle layers. Both can be blended in one session.

Can I have a massage if I am injured or in pain?

Often yes, but it depends on the condition. Tell me about any pain, injury or medical condition when you book so we can assess whether massage is appropriate and adapt safely.

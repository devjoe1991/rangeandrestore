Before the race

Pre-race preparation

In the weeks before a race, the goal is to address anything that has been building up through high-mileage training. Tight ITBs, sore glutes, stiff calves, and overworked hip flexors are all common at this stage.

During taper, sessions become lighter, focusing on flushing out fatigue and keeping the legs feeling responsive rather than heavy. The timing matters, and Carlos works with your race date in mind.

After the race

Post-race recovery

After a marathon the body has been through a significant load. DOMS tends to peak 48 to 72 hours after the finish line. A well-timed recovery session, a few days post-race, helps clear that faster and reduces the chance of tightness turning into a longer-term problem.

The Recovery Suite is also available for post-race days: Normatec compression is particularly effective for heavy, swollen legs after long-distance effort.

Would you share your experience on Google?

Most people who find us for the first time do it through Google, and the first thing they read is what other clients have said. For someone in pain, or unsure about booking a treatment they have never had before, a few honest words from a real client can be what helps them decide.

As a small clinic, reviews make a real difference to us too. They keep the treatment rooms busy, they support our therapists, and they mean a lot to the person who treated you. We read every one.

If you have not left a review yet, we would be very grateful if you could spare a couple of minutes. If you already have, thank you. You can skip this part.

Not sure what to write? You could mention:

What you first came in for. Back pain, a running injury, tension from desk work, or simply wanting to feel better.

Whether the treatment helped. How the session felt and whether it was what you needed.

What changed afterwards. Less pain, easier movement, better sleep, or getting back to training.

The therapist you saw. Mentioning them by name means a lot to them.

Whether you would recommend us. Your therapist, or Range and Restore generally, to a friend or colleague.

A few sentences is plenty. It does not need to be long or polished, and an honest review is worth more to us than a perfect one.

# Range and Restore — Local Problem-Based Outreach Playbook

*Archway, North London (N19 5LB). Last updated July 2026.*

The idea: lead with **the audience's problem**, not our service. Every pitch below opens with a pain the target's people already have (sore legs, bad backs, post-op swelling) and offers something useful first. Links, referrals and footfall follow.

---

## 1. Priority targets (by problem cluster)

| # | Target type | Their people's problem | What we offer them | Local examples to find |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Running clubs & parkrun** | Shin splints, tight calves, sore legs, race recovery | A members' recovery talk + a club discount code; a "race-week recovery" guide | Highgate Harriers, Dartmouth Park Runners, Highbury parkrun, Finsbury Park parkrun, Hampstead Heath parkrun |
| 2 | **Gyms / CrossFit / F45 / PT studios** | DOMS, tweaks, mobility limits | "Recovery corner" partnership, a member offer, Recovery Suite (sauna + Normatec) intro | Independent gyms & boxes across N19/N7/N6/N4 |
| 3 | **Cycling clubs & bike shops** | Lower-back & neck pain, tight hip flexors, heavy legs | Cyclist recovery guide + shop leaflet/discount | Local clubs; bike shops in Archway/Holloway/Highgate |
| 4 | **GPs, physios & osteopaths** | Patients needing hands-on soft-tissue work, post-op MLD, sciatica | A clear referral pathway + same-building imaging via Reliable Scan | Nearby GP surgeries, physio & osteo clinics; **Reliable Scan is the anchor** |
| 5 | **Offices & co-working spaces** | Desk back pain, stiff necks, WFH aches | A lunch-and-learn "desk posture" session + staff wellbeing offer | Co-working/offices in Archway, Islington, Camden |
| 6 | **Yoga / Pilates studios** | Tight hips/hamstrings, recovery, wellbeing | Cross-referral (they stretch, we release), joint workshop | Local studios (e.g. Alaric Yoga — already a community partner) |
| 7 | **Local Facebook groups / Nextdoor** | "Anyone recommend a good sports massage?" | Genuinely helpful answers to problem posts (not spam) | Archway/N19, Tufnell Park, Highgate, Crouch End community groups |
| 8 | **Marathon/event organisers** | Runners needing pre/post-race recovery | On-the-day massage stand or a partner offer | Autumn marathon/half season (London ballot, Royal Parks, local 10Ks) |

**Warm start:** #4 (Reliable Scan, same building) and #6 (existing community partners) are the fastest first wins — relationships already exist.

## 2. What to lead with (matched to each blog post)

Point every outreach touch at the **problem post** that matches the audience, so the recipient gets useful content, not a sales page:

- Runners / parkrun → **Shin splints**, **Achilles**, **Tight hamstrings**, **Sports massage for runners**
- Cyclists → **Cycling recovery**
- Gyms / PTs → **Sore legs / Normatec compression**, **Recovery Suite**
- Offices / WFH → **Desk posture**, **Office worker massage**, **Tennis elbow / RSI**
- GPs / physios → **Sciatica**, **MLD / post-op** service page
- General public → **Sports massage in Archway: local guide**

## 4. Assets to prepare (once)
- **Referral cards** for GPs/physios (name, credentials, Reliable Scan same-building line, QR to booking).
- **A5 club/gym flyer** with a QR to the matching problem post + a discount code.
- **A "race-week recovery" one-pager** (PDF) — the most link-worthy freebie for running clubs.
- **A member discount code** per partner (trackable, so you know which outreach converts).

## 5. Measure it
- Give each partner a **unique discount code or booking link** → attribute sessions.
- In Google Search Console, watch impressions/clicks on the problem posts as backlinks land.
- Track referral sources in Jane (ask "how did you hear about us?").
- Review monthly: which cluster is converting → do more of that outreach.

## 6. Cadence (realistic for a small clinic)
- **Weekly:** answer 2–3 genuine problem posts in local groups; 1 new partner email.
- **Monthly:** one in-person visit (club talk, office session, or GP drop-in).
- **Seasonal:** ramp running-club outreach ahead of the autumn marathon season; offices in January (New-Year aches) and September (back-to-desk).

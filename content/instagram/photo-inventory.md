## Before you post any photo with a person in it

1. **Client consent.** Every treatment photo with an identifiable client needs recorded consent for social
   use specifically — website consent is not automatically Instagram consent. `IMG_1309`, `IMG_1380` and
   `IMG_1387` are anonymous (no face) which lowers but does not remove the requirement.
2. **The course photos (`IMG_1262`, `IMG_1112`)** show other students and models. Get the course
   provider's OK before posting, and don't tag anyone who hasn't agreed.
3. **Reliable Scan branding** appears in the reception and exterior shots. That's a genuine partnership,
   so it's fine — but keep the framing collaborative rather than implying the scan clinic is yours.

## Practical cropping notes

- The reception (`IMG_1552`) and exterior shots are **very wide**. At 4:5 you lose a lot of the sides —
  crop toward the roller banner in the reception shot and toward the Range and Restore window graphic
  on the exterior shot.
- `sports-massage-soft-tissue-therapy-…jpeg` is 16:9. Cropping to 4:5 tightens onto Carlos and the client's
  back, which is the stronger frame anyway.
- The sauna photo is 1600×1132 — at 4:5 you'll crop to roughly 1600×2000, which it can't fill.
  **Crop to 4:5 from the centre-right** (cabin + mirror) and accept ~1132px height, or run it at 1:1.
- All Gallery `.jpeg` files at 5712px wide are phone-camera originals. Export from Canva at
  "Download → PNG → 2x off" — they're already far above what IG needs, and IG recompresses anyway.

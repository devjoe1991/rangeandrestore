## Cadence

**3 grid posts a week + stories most days.** That's sustainable for a clinic and enough to stay visible.
Don't start at 5 a week and stop in a fortnight.

| Day | Slot | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | 07:30–08:30 | Education / condition (Pillar D) — people are at desks and searching |
| Wednesday | 12:00–13:00 | Recovery Suite, Normatec or packages (A / B / C) |
| Saturday | 09:00–10:00 | Local, team, sport-specific or proof (E / F / G) |

Reels can go any day — they don't compete with the grid slots. Aim for one a week.

Best times are a starting point, not gospel. After four weeks, check Insights → Audience → Most active times
and move the slots to match your actual followers.

## Publishing workflow

1. **Build in Canva** from the template named on the post (T1–T5). Brand kit set up per `content/canva_plan.md`.
2. **Export** — Download → PNG. Don't upscale; Instagram recompresses everything anyway.
3. **Check the crop** — preview the profile grid thumbnail. If the headline or price is cut off, move it inward.
4. **Run the pre-publish checklist** at the end of `content-pack.md`. The price check is the one that matters.
5. **Schedule** in Meta Business Suite, or post natively. Native posting still gets marginally better reach,
   but consistency beats the margin — if scheduling is what makes it happen, schedule it.
6. **Alt text** — Advanced settings → Write alt text. Every post. Non-negotiable.
7. **Location tag** — "Archway, London" on every post. It's a real local ranking signal.
8. **First hour** — reply to every comment. Comment replies in the first hour do more for a post
   than the hashtags do.

## Link in bio

You only get one link, and the captions reference several destinations. Options:

- **Simplest:** point it at `rangeandrestore.co.uk` and update the caption to name the page
  ("full write-up on the blog") — visitors navigate from the homepage.
- **Better:** a link-in-bio page listing Book on Jane · Recovery Suite · 3D Sauna Tour · Blog · Contact.
  Given the site is already Next.js, this could just be a `/links` route styled with the existing
  brand tokens rather than a third-party tool — no extra subscription, no external domain in the
  analytics, and it keeps the Google Ads / GA4 cross-domain tracking clean.

Whichever you pick, **change the link when the post changes.** A caption saying "link in bio" pointing at
the wrong page is the most common way clinics lose the booking.

## What to measure

Check monthly, not daily.

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Saves | Insights → per post | Education posts working. Saves beat likes for this account |
| Shares | Insights → per post | Local reach — someone sent it to a friend in N19 |
| Profile visits → link taps | Insights → Account | The only number that ties to bookings |
| Reach from non-followers | Insights → per post | Hashtags and format working |
| Top posts by format | Insights → Content | Tells you whether to lean carousel or Reel |

Expect carousels (D-pillar) to lead on saves, Reels to lead on reach, and poster-style price cards (T2)
to lead on link taps. If that pattern doesn't hold after two months, shift the mix toward whatever is.
